📍 What this assessment covers
The noise that blurs your desires comes in two layers. This assessment focuses on ① social-structural noise.
  • Social monitoring pressure — noise that makes you read others' reactions first
  • Comparison pressure — noise that makes comparison kick in before your own desire
  • Comparison exposure — noise from what social media algorithms keep showing you
② Psychological noise (inner patterns formed during upbringing — perfectionism, dependence on pride, emotion avoidance, etc.) will be released as a separate assessment soon.
This assessment is built on Heo & Park (2013) Nunchi Scale, Festinger (1954) Social Comparison Theory, and Charry & Tessitore (2021) research on social media conformity effects.
